# Interview Preparation Workflow

1. Load job and company context.
2. Load selected resume version.
3. Retrieve relevant evidence.
4. Identify likely question areas.
5. Generate role-specific questions.
6. Generate evidence-grounded answer outlines.
7. Identify weak or unsupported areas.
8. Build mock interview sequence.
9. Capture feedback.
10. Feed useful feedback into future preparation.
