# Resume Tailoring Prompt Contract

Tailor from the evidence bank, not from the job description alone.

Rules:

- Preserve truth.
- Preserve seniority.
- Preserve actual titles and dates.
- Use JD terminology only where accurate.
- Prioritize relevant outcomes.
- De-emphasize unrelated material.
- Do not invent metrics, tools, industries, clients, P&L, or leadership scope.
- Maintain readable ATS-safe formatting.
- Produce a change log and evidence map.
