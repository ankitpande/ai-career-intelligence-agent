# Product Requirements

## Objective

Build a reusable AI career intelligence system that helps a senior professional make evidence-based decisions and execute applications without fabricating experience.

## Functional requirements

### Career evidence
- Store career facts, achievements, projects, skills, leadership examples, and supporting evidence.
- Track evidence status.
- Preserve source and traceability.
- Maintain pending questions without repeatedly asking the same question.

### Job intelligence
- Ingest multiple job-input formats.
- Separate extracted facts from inference.
- Structure mandatory, preferred, and nice-to-have requirements.
- Preserve source and date.

### Fitment
- Overall fit score.
- Category scores.
- Explanation for each category.
- Evidence, missing evidence, assumptions, and gaps.
- Automatic descriptive priority flag when predefined conditions are met.

### Tailoring
- JD-relevant ordering.
- Truthful terminology.
- No keyword stuffing.
- Seniority preservation.
- Change log.
- Evidence map.

### Applications
- Field-by-field answer generation.
- Confidence and source attached to each answer.
- Explicit confirmation for uncertain answers.
- Never auto-submit.

### Career learning
- Aggregate skill gaps across multiple relevant roles.
- Build sequenced learning plans.
- Connect learning to tangible evidence such as projects or work examples.

### Interview preparation
- Role-specific questions.
- Resume evidence questions.
- Business and leadership cases.
- Honest handling of missing direct experience.
