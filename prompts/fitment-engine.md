# Fitment Engine Prompt Contract

Assess the relationship between a candidate's evidence and a specific job requirement.

For each category return:

- score
- supporting evidence
- missing evidence
- assumptions
- explicit gap type

Gap types:

- EXPERIENCE_GAP
- SKILL_GAP
- EVIDENCE_GAP
- DOMAIN_GAP
- QUALIFICATION_GAP
- LOCATION_WORK_MODEL_GAP

Do not treat lack of resume wording as proof of lack of skill.

Priority flags are descriptive system outputs, not career decisions.
